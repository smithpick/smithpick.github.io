Tech Board

No login needed. Kids follow the pictures on the board.

  1. On the Newline board, tap the Tech Board bookmark. No bookmark? Scan the code or type the link.
  2. Tap Full screen (top right).
  3. Under This week, tap today's item.
  4. In a guide, tap Next for each step. Tap Home (bottom left) to go back.
  5. Timer: tap a minute button, then Start.

Can't get it on the board? Scan the code with your phone and show kids the pictures. Tap Big for one picture at a time.

Links that open a new tab may ask for a Google login. Skip those.
